## Attribution

Named-individual attribution is **opt-in**. Contributors who prefer
role-based credit (“emburden project”, “external collaborator”) list
under those labels; contributors who want personal credit ask for it at
`info@emburden.org` and are added here. `git log --format='%an <%ae>'`
is the authoritative raw record of who contributed which lines.

Foundation officers are listed in the Foundation’s own
`emergi-foundation/.github/GOVERNANCE.md` (a governance-genre doc, which
does name officers per standard nonprofit practice).

The maintainer’s ORCID for this repo:
[0000-0002-6628-1389](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-6628-1389).

## AI-assisted development disclosure

Portions of this codebase and its accompanying paper were developed with
AI coding-assistant collaboration (Anthropic’s Claude Code, model family
Opus / Sonnet across the 4.x / 5.x releases, 2026). Human authors have
reviewed and are responsible for all code, analysis, and scholarly
claims committed to the repository. Per-commit AI-collaboration tagging
was retired on 2026-09-19 in favour of this project-level disclosure;
historical commits before that date carry `Co-Authored-By: Claude ...`
metadata in `git log`.

## Cited academic work

Academic sources drawn on by the code and paper (e.g. Odum 1996 on
emergy accounting; Deming and Stephan 1940 and Beckman 1996 on iterative
proportional fitting; Hruschka, Gerkey and Hadley 2015 in Bull WHO on
absolute-wealth estimation; Reed and Jorgensen 2004 on
double-Pareto-lognormal distributions) are cited in the relevant paper
or vignette. Citation of published methods is by publication, not by
personal name, so cited-author names in scholarly references are exempt
from the ecosystem’s name-scrubbing policy.
