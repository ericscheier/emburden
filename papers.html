Publication strategy

Each paper answers a different question. Reviewers at each stage can’t reject on grounds already peer-reviewed upstream.
  • JSS umbrella establishes tool validity → downstream papers cite it
  • JOSS individual papers establish package-level DOIs
  • Scientific Data establishes dataset validity as a citable object
  • Joule applies the validated tool + data to a global synthesis
  • Science Policy Forum condenses the finding into a policy claim
  • Nature Energy builds a causal argument on the validated dataset

Timeline: JSS + JOSS Wave 1 submissions this month; Joule + Science draft-review this month, submission next; Nature Energy in ~12 months after EM-DAT + GDELT pulls complete.
